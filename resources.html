Quick Reference

PAN (Permanent Account Number) is a 10-character ID issued by the Income Tax Department. It's used to track your income and taxes, and is needed to file returns, open a bank account, or register a business.
GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a tax added to the sale of most goods and services. If your business turnover crosses a certain limit, you must register for GST and collect/report this tax.
ITR (Income Tax Return) is the yearly form you file to report your income and pay any tax owed. Filing it can also help when applying for loans or visas, even if you owe little or no tax.
A simplified way to calculate tax for small businesses. Instead of tracking every expense, a fixed percentage of your turnover is treated as profit, and you pay tax on that — no detailed books required.
MSME stands for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises. Registering as one (via Udyam) is free and can unlock loans, subsidies, and other government support for your business.
